Project Overview

This project documents the repair and modification of a Kenwood TR9000 transceiver. Tasks include connector upgrades, power supply modifications, physical repairs, and frequency tuning.

RF Connector Conversion (SO239 → BNC)

The original SO239 connector was replaced with a BNC for easier connection to test equipment.

DC Power Connector Upgrade (Powerpole)

The original DC barrel connector was replaced with Powerpole connectors for reliability and safety.

Repair of Device Feet

The rubber feet were damaged; we replaced them with new pads to stabilize the unit.

1750Hz Tone Generator Repair

Initial repair attempts were unsuccessful. This demonstrates the challenges of working with older analog circuits.

Frequency Alignment

After all repairs and modifications, the transceiver was aligned to standard frequencies to ensure proper operation.
